About

I'm a policy researcher and, since March 2025, a Policy Analyst at the Canada West Foundation, where I research and write on energy and climate policy, housing and construction, and technology and digital infrastructure for audiences across government, industry, and the public.

I began in law, earning a B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) and qualifying to practise law in India. I then joined the National Institute of Public Finance and Policy in New Delhi, one of India's premier think tanks under the aegis of the Ministry of Finance, as a Research Fellow. To continue learning, I moved to Canada for a Master of Public Policy at the School of Public Policy, University of Calgary, graduating in October 2024, and worked as a Policy Consultant with Actuo Consultancy before joining Canada West.

I'm driven by a desire to make a meaningful impact, particularly in competition, climate, and energy policy, where I hope to use my interdisciplinary background to address complex societal challenges, both locally and globally.

Focus areas

Energy & climate

Bioenergy with carbon capture and storage (BECCS) and the policy barriers to deploying it in Western Canada.

Competition policy

Competition law and policy, from the 2024 greenwashing amendments to the Competition Act (Bill C-59) to how climate concerns can be weighed in merger review, the subject of my MPP capstone, Considering Climate Concerns in Corporate Merger Review under the Canadian Competition Act: A Policy Perspective.

Technology & digital infrastructure

The policies and projects shaping Canada's digital infrastructure, tracked monthly through Frontiers in Tech.

Housing & construction

Research and analysis on housing and the construction sector for government, industry, and public audiences.
